## Colors

The mark is a glowing neon wordmark/monogram on a near-black field.

| Role | Hex | Swatch |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#040310` | ⬛ |
| Neon Cyan | `#00F0FF` | 🩵 |

Near-black `#040310` is the primary background. Neon Cyan `#00F0FF` is the single accent and the color of the mark itself, rendered with a soft outer glow.

## Typography

- **Wordmark / monogram:** [Rubik](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Rubik), weight **700 (Bold)**.
- **Tagline:** monospace, letter-spaced — "CREATIVE VENTURE STUDIO".

## Usage

- **Background:** The logo is built for dark backgrounds. Keep it on `#040310` or a very dark surface.
- **Clear space:** Leave padding of at least the cap-height of the "U" around the mark.
- **Minimum size:** Don't render the square mark below 32×32 px (below that, use `favicon.svg` / `favicon.ico`).
- **Glow:** The neon glow is part of the mark. Don't flatten it to a hard-edged solid when the medium can show the glow.
- **Don't:** recolor, rotate, stretch, add extra effects/shadows, place on busy or light backgrounds, or alter the wordmark lettering.
